## 📖 What Is Alex's Caves?

**Alex's Caves** adds **six rare cave biomes** hidden beneath the Overworld. Each biome is a massive underground region with its own unique blocks, items, mobs, and mechanics [citation:9][citation:12].

The mod is designed around **exploration and discovery**. You won't find these caves by digging randomly. You need to locate an **Underground Cabin**, loot a **Cave Compendium** and **Cave Tablets**, then decipher the tablets at a **Spelunkery Table** to craft maps pointing to each biome [citation:7][citation:12].

Every biome has its own atmosphere — particles, fog, lighting, and sound design — that makes it feel like a lost world waiting to be found [citation:5].

**Key highlights:**
- ✅ **Six unique cave biomes** — Magnetic, Primordial, Toxic, Abyssal, Forlorn, Candy
- ✅ **43 unique mobs** — Dinosaurs, deep-sea horrors, radioactive mutants, gummy critters
- ✅ **Colossal bosses** — Massive creatures at the end of each biome's progression
- ✅ **350+ blocks** — Building materials, decorative blocks, and special mechanics
- ✅ **Gear progression** — Unique armor, tools, and weapons per biome
- ✅ **In-game guide** — The Cave Compendium documents everything

## ✨ The Six Cave Biomes

### 🧲 Magnetic Caves
Sheer iron-bearing rock under a haze of floating metal. Ferrouslimes ooze between pillars, magnetrons hover and pull, and mine guardians defend ancient ruins. Magnetism is a **real mechanic** here — you'll repel and attract blocks, mobs, and even yourself. The Galena Gauntlet turns that force into a weapon [citation:9].

### 🦖 Primordial Caves
A lost world of ferns and mossy stone where the dinosaurs never stopped. Tremorsaurs, Grottoceratops, Atlatitans, and Subterranodons range across the caverns. Amber preserves what used to live here — and something **considerably larger** than any of them is still down there [citation:9][citation:16].

### ☢️ Toxic Caves
An irradiated wasteland of glowing waste and rusted concrete. **Radiation is a status effect** you have to manage — which is what the Hazmat Suit is for. Gammaroaches, Nucleepers, Brainiacs, and Raycats infest it. The Nuclear Furnace smelts on uranium. And if things go badly wrong, the **Tremorzilla** is what comes out of the dark [citation:9][citation:16].

### 🌊 Abyssal Chasm
A flooded trench that goes far deeper than the ocean above it, lit by lanternfish and the ruins of a civilization that is **still occupied**. The Deep Ones — and their knights and mages — do not want visitors. Bring a diving suit, and expect the pressure and the dark to be the real enemies [citation:9].

### 🏚️ Forlorn Hollows
Gothic spires, thornwood, and bridges over a canyon you cannot see the bottom of. Watchers, Vespers, Gloomoths, and the Forsaken haunt it; the Licowitch keeps a tower here. This is where the mod's **darkness mechanics** live, and where the Spelunkery Table becomes essential [citation:9].

### 🍬 Candy Cavity
Nougat and hardened sugar, gum worms, sweetish fish, caramel cubes, and gummy bears. **Gingerbread men** have built an entire town down here — and they are not friendly. It is exactly as strange as it sounds, and it is a genuine biome with a full progression, not a joke [citation:9].
